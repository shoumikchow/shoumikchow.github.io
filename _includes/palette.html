The trigger that opens it by pointer lives in the layout's header, next to the nav it stands in for. A native opened with showModal(), which is most of the accessibility for free: the page behind goes inert, Esc is wired, and focus goes back to wherever it was when the dialog closes. {%- endcomment -%}
